## Our commitment

At Sweet & Cinnful, we believe everyone deserves a sweet experience — online and in every bite. We are committed to ensuring that our website, sweetandcinnful.com, is accessible to people of all abilities, including those who use assistive technologies such as screen readers, screen magnifiers, voice recognition software, and keyboard-only navigation.

## Conformance status

We strive to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA, published by the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C). These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible for people with a wide range of disabilities, including visual, auditory, physical, speech, cognitive, language, learning, and neurological differences.

Sweet & Cinnful is a small, home-based bakery in Lexington, South Carolina. As a solo baker and business owner, I am continually working to improve the accessibility of my website and make the ordering experience welcoming for every customer.

## Measures we take

To support accessibility, Sweet & Cinnful:

- Uses descriptive alternative text for photos of cakes, cookies, cupcakes, and other baked goods
- Maintains sufficient color contrast between text and backgrounds
- Provides visible keyboard focus indicators for users who navigate without a mouse
- Includes a "Skip to main content" link for screen reader users
- Structures pages with proper heading hierarchy and semantic HTML
- Designs forms with clearly visible labels and accessible error messages
- Tests the website on mobile devices and with assistive technologies
- Honors user preferences for reduced motion

## Known limitations

Despite our best efforts, some areas of the website may not yet be fully accessible. Known limitations may include:

- Third-party embedded content (such as linked social media pages) that is outside our direct control
- Older photos that may not yet have complete alt-text descriptions — we are updating these on an ongoing basis

We are actively working to resolve these issues and welcome feedback from users who encounter any barriers.

## Alternative ordering options

If you are unable to complete a custom order through our online form, you are always welcome to place an order by emailing us directly. We will gladly walk you through the process and gather your details through whichever method works best for you.

## Ongoing improvements

Accessibility is not a one-time project — it is an ongoing commitment. We periodically review our website using automated tools, manual testing, and real user feedback to identify and address areas for improvement as the site grows.

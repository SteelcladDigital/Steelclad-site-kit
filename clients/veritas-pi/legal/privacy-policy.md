Veritas Investigative Group ("Veritas," "we," "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information this website (veritaspigroup.com) collects, how we use it and the choices you have. It covers the website only. Information you share with Veritas during an engagement is handled under the terms of that engagement and applicable law.

## Information you give us

When you use the contact form, call or email us, you may give us:

- Your first and last name
- Your phone number and email address
- A description of your situation or request

Please share only what is needed for a first conversation. Do not send highly sensitive details (such as Social Security numbers, financial account numbers or medical information) through the website form. Contacting Veritas through this website does not create a client relationship.

## Information collected automatically

Like most websites, this site collects some information automatically when you visit:

- **Basic technical data** such as your IP address, browser type, device type and the pages you view. Our hosting provider records this in standard server logs for security and reliability.
- **How you found the site.** If you arrive from an advertisement or a tracked link, the site may remember the campaign details (such as the source and campaign name) in your browser and include them with a form submission, so we know which channels bring inquiries.
- **Analytics.** We use Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site, such as which pages are viewed, form submissions and phone-number clicks. These tools use cookies and similar technologies and report information in aggregate.

## How we use information

We use the information described above to:

- Respond to your inquiry and discuss whether and how Veritas can assist
- Schedule consultations and communicate with you about your request
- Operate, secure and improve this website
- Understand which pages and channels are most useful to visitors
- Comply with legal obligations, including those that apply to licensed private investigators in South Carolina

## How we share information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with others for their own marketing.

We share information only with service providers that help us run the website and respond to you, and only as needed for that purpose. These include our website host, the service that delivers contact-form submissions to our email (Web3Forms) and Google (Tag Manager and Analytics). We may also disclose information when required by law, subpoena or other legal process, or to protect the rights and safety of Veritas, our clients or others.

## Cookies and your choices

You can set your browser to block or delete cookies, and you can clear the site's stored data at any time. Google offers an opt-out browser add-on for Google Analytics at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout. Blocking cookies will not prevent you from using the site or contacting us.

## How long we keep information

We keep contact-form messages and related correspondence for as long as needed to respond to your inquiry, maintain our business records and meet legal and licensing requirements. Analytics data is retained according to our Google Analytics settings.

## Security

We use reasonable measures to protect the information you send us, including encrypted (HTTPS) connections to this website. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Children's privacy

This website is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

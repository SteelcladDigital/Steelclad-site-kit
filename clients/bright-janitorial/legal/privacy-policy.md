Bright Janitorial Services LLC ("Bright Janitorial Services," "we," "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through brightjs.net, how we use it, and the choices you have.

## Information we collect

**Information you give us.** When you request a quote or contact us through the website, we collect the details you enter: your name, phone number, email address, company or facility name, service location, the type of facility and service you're interested in, how often you need service, and your message.

**Information collected automatically.** Like most websites, we use Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site, such as pages viewed, how you arrived (for example, a search engine or an ad), approximate location, and device and browser type. These tools use cookies or similar technologies. When you send a form, we also record the page you sent it from and how you first found the site (for example, the referring website or campaign tags in the link) so we know which marketing is working.

## How we use your information

- To respond to your request, prepare quotes and proposals, and provide our services
- To contact you by phone, text or email about your request or service
- To improve our website and understand which advertising and marketing work
- To keep records required for our business, tax and legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information.

## Text messages

If you check the consent box on our form, we may contact you by text message about your request. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help. **We do not share or sell your mobile number or text-messaging consent with third parties or affiliates for their marketing purposes.**

## Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that help us run the business and the website, and only as needed for that purpose:

- **Web3Forms**, which delivers website form submissions to our email
- **Google** (Tag Manager and Analytics), for website measurement
- **Cloudflare**, which hosts and secures this website

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights.

## Our commitment

At Palmetto Pool Solutions, LLC, we believe that every homeowner in Lexington and Richland Counties deserves access to reliable, professional pool service — and that extends to our website, too. We are committed to ensuring that palmettopoolsolutions.com is accessible to people of all abilities, including those who use assistive technologies such as screen readers, screen magnifiers, voice recognition software, and keyboard-only navigation.

Just as we treat every pool as if our own children were swimming in it, we want every visitor to our website — regardless of ability — to feel welcomed, informed, and confident reaching out to us.

## Conformance status

We strive to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA, published by the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C). These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible for people with a wide range of disabilities, including visual, auditory, physical, speech, cognitive, language, learning, and neurological differences.

As a family-owned, locally operated business, we continually work to improve the accessibility of our website and phone-based service as our company grows.

## Measures we take

To support accessibility, Palmetto Pool Solutions:

- Uses descriptive alternative text for photos of pools, equipment, and service work
- Maintains strong color contrast between text and backgrounds
- Provides visible keyboard focus indicators for users who navigate without a mouse
- Includes a "Skip to main content" link for screen reader users
- Structures pages with proper heading hierarchy and semantic HTML
- Designs contact forms with clearly visible labels and accessible error messages
- Honors user preferences for reduced motion
- Provides our phone number prominently — we answer our phone, so customers who prefer voice contact never have to rely on the website alone

## Known limitations

Despite our best efforts, some areas of the website may not yet be fully accessible. Known limitations may include:

- Third-party content or links (such as Google reviews or social media pages) that are outside our direct control
- Older service photos that may not yet have complete alt-text descriptions — we are updating these on an ongoing basis

We are actively working to resolve these issues and welcome feedback from users who encounter any barriers.

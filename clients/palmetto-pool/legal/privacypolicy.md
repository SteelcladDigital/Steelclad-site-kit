Palmetto Pool Solutions, LLC ("Palmetto Pool Solutions," "we," "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through palmettopoolsolutions.com, how we use it, and the choices you have.

## Information we collect

**Information you give us.** When you request a free pool evaluation or contact us through the website, we collect the details you enter: your name, phone number, email address, city, pool type, the service you're interested in, your message, and whether you agreed to receive text messages.

**Information collected automatically.** Like most websites, we use Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site, such as pages viewed, how you arrived (for example, a search engine or an ad), approximate location, and device and browser type. These tools use cookies or similar technologies. When you send a form, we also record the page you sent it from and how you first found the site (for example, the referring website or campaign tags in the link) so we know which marketing is working.

## How we use your information

- To respond to your request, schedule your evaluation and provide our services
- To contact you by phone, text or email about your request or service
- To improve our website and understand which advertising and marketing work
- To keep records required for our business, tax and legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information.

## Text messages (SMS)

If you check the text-message consent box on our form, or text us first, we may text you about your service request, scheduling and account updates. Message frequency varies, and message and data rates may apply. Reply **STOP** to opt out or **HELP** for help. Consent is not a condition of purchase. **Mobile opt-in data and consent are never shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes.** See our [Terms of Service](/termsandconditions/) for the full SMS terms.

## Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that help us run the business and the website, and only as needed for that purpose:

- **Web3Forms**, which delivers website form submissions to our email
- **Google** (Tag Manager and Analytics), for website measurement
- **Cloudflare**, which hosts and secures this website

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights.

Hiring a pool service company sounds simple. You Google a few names, check some reviews, and pick one. But if you've ever ended up with a no-show technician, a pool that somehow got worse after a "service visit," or a company that stopped returning calls after the first month, you already know it's not that simple.

Here's what to actually look for when hiring a pool service company in the Lexington and Columbia area.

## 1. Are They Licensed, Certified, and Insured?

This is the first question to ask — and most homeowners never do.

In South Carolina, pool service companies should hold a **Certified Pool Operator (CPO)** certification. This means they've been formally trained in water chemistry, equipment operation, safety standards, and health code compliance. It's not just a piece of paper — it's the difference between a technician who guesses at your water chemistry and one who understands why your pH affects everything else.

Beyond certification, ask about **liability insurance**. A technician working in your backyard around expensive equipment, electrical systems, and a body of water should be insured. If they're not and something goes wrong, you could be on the hook.

## 2. Do They Communicate Clearly and Consistently?

This one separates good pool companies from frustrating ones fast.

Ask yourself: when you call or text, do they respond the same day? After your first service visit, do you know what was done? If a problem is found at your pool, do they tell you about it — or just fix it without asking?

Look for a company that leaves **written documentation after every visit** — a door hanger, a text summary, an app update — something that tells you what was tested, what was adjusted, and what (if anything) needs attention. You shouldn't have to wonder whether your pool was actually serviced.

This is a standard part of every Palmetto Pool Solutions visit. You'll always know what was done at your pool, even when you weren't home to see it.

## 3. Are They Actually Local?

There's a big difference between a local owner-operator and a national franchise with rotating technicians and a call center two states away.

Local companies know the specific challenges of **South Carolina pool ownership** — the long swim season, [the summer heat that accelerates algae growth](/services/green-to-clean-pool-recovery/), the red clay that stains pool surfaces, and the neighborhood-level quirks that affect water chemistry. They're also more accountable. When the same person services your pool every week, and you can call a local number to reach a real human, the quality of service reflects that relationship.

Ask who will actually be servicing your pool. If the answer is "whoever is available that week," that's worth knowing upfront.

## 4. What's Actually Included in the Service?

Not all pool service is created equal. When you're comparing quotes, make sure you're comparing the same scope of work.

A legitimate [weekly service](/services/weekly-pool-maintenance/) should include water chemistry testing **and** balancing — not just testing. It should include brushing the walls and steps, not just skimming the surface. It should include an equipment check, not just a chemical drop.

Get specifics. A low monthly rate that only covers a chemical check and a quick skim isn't a deal — it's a setup for bigger problems down the road.

## 5. Do They Have Real Local Reviews?

Google reviews from real customers in your area tell you more than any sales pitch. Look for patterns, not just star ratings. Are customers mentioning reliability? Communication? Do they mention the technician by name? Those details signal a company that builds real relationships.

Be cautious of companies with dozens of generic five-star reviews and no specific details. And pay attention to how a company **responds** to negative reviews — that tells you everything about how they handle problems.

The best pool service company in Lexington isn't necessarily the cheapest or the biggest. It's the one that shows up consistently, communicates clearly, knows what they're doing, and treats your pool like it matters.

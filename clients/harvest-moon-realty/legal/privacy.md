Harvest Moon Realty ("Harvest Moon Realty," "we," "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through harvestmoonrealty.com, how we use it, and the choices you have.

## You can search without signing up

You do not have to create an account or give us your name, email address or phone number to search home and land listings on this website. We only receive your contact details if you choose to send them to us.

## Information we collect

**Information you give us.** When you contact us through the website, we collect the details you enter: your name, phone number, email address and your message, such as the kind of home or land you're looking for, the area and your timing.

**Information collected automatically.** Like most websites, we use tools that record how visitors use the site, such as pages viewed, how you arrived (for example, a search engine, social media or an ad), approximate location, and device and browser type. These tools use cookies or similar technologies. When you send a form, we also record the page you sent it from and how you found the site so we know which marketing is working.

**Listing search.** Our home and land search is provided by an IDX service connected to the Consolidated Multiple Listing Service (CMLS). The provider may use cookies to run the search (for example, to remember your filters). Registration is not required to use it.

## How we use your information

- To respond to your questions and help you buy or sell real estate
- To contact you by phone, text or email about your request
- To improve our website and understand which advertising and marketing work
- To keep records required for our business, licensing, tax and legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information. We do not send you marketing text messages without your separate permission.

## Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that help us run the business and the website, and only as needed for that purpose:

- **HighLevel (GoHighLevel)**, our customer relationship and messaging system, which receives website form submissions
- **Our IDX listing search provider**, which runs the MLS search on this site
- **Google** (Tag Manager and Analytics), for website measurement
- **Cloudflare**, which hosts and secures this website

When you're buying or selling, we may also share information you approve with the other parties to your transaction, such as lenders, inspectors and closing attorneys. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights.

## Your choices

- You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and you can opt out of Google Analytics with Google's browser add-on.
- You can reply STOP to any text message from us to stop texts.
- You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information you've sent us by emailing [barry@harvestmoonrealty.com](mailto:barry@harvestmoonrealty.com) or calling [(803) 309-9521](tel:+18033099521).

## Data retention and security

We keep inquiries and client records as long as needed to serve you and meet our legal, licensing and business obligations. We use reasonable safeguards, but no website or email system is completely secure.

## Children

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.
